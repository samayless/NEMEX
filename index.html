NANOPHOTONICS AND ENERGY MATERIAL EXPLORATION LABORATORY

NEMEX studies how light behaves inside nanostructured and photonic materials, then engineers that behaviour into working technology — solar-driven desalination, photocatalytic hydrogen production, and radiative cooling that needs no electricity.

Supervisor

The researcher leading the lab's scientific direction.

Dr. Satish Laxman Shinde

Assistant Professor, Department of Physics, IIT Hyderabad

Ph.D.Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru
OfficeRoom PH-409, Physics Building, IITH

Dr. Shinde directs the Nanophotonics & Energy Material Exploration Laboratory. His research sits at the intersection of advanced nanomaterials and renewable energy generation, drawing on a background in applied physics and nanophotonics to address pressing global challenges.

His primary interests span nanophotonics, energy materials, photothermal energy harvesting, photocatalysis, and semiconducting devices — bridging fundamental light–matter interaction at the nanoscale with scalable, real-world technologies.
